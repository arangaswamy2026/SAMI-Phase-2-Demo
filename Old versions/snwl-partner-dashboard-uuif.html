🎯Concept 3 — Tenant Scorecard: Partners think in tenants, not products. Each tenant gets a card with a composite health score (aggregated from NSM + WNM + CC + CSE). Cards are sortable by health score, revenue tier, or alert count. Ideal for monthly review prep and partner QBRs.
📐Layout: Filter bar + search. Grid of tenant cards (4-col) with health score ring, product status pills, device count, and top alert. Click-through to tenant detail. Inspired by Quantix LLM dashboard 4-col metric grid.

📐Layout: Left = daily action queue with priority lanes. Center-top = 6 KPI count blocks. Center-bottom = event timeline. Right = license renewal tracker. Kealthy Hospital two-panel reference for the sidebar + main split.
