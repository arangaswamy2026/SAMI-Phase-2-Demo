Design Takeaways vs. SAMI Insights Current State

What SAMI Does Well

3-panel layout (sidebar → list → detail) matches industry standard for alert triage.
Category-based grouping with count badges is the right mental model for MSP operators.
"SAMI Recommends" callout box is well-positioned — consistent with CrowdStrike's Charlotte AI strip.
Severity tags (Critical) + category tags (CVE) on cards match PagerDuty's priority badge pattern.

Opportunities to Explore

Structured sections in AI callout — add explicit "Risk Assessment" and "Remediation Steps" subheadings inside the SAMI Recommends box (Wiz pattern).
Evidence panel — show scope (tenant list, device count) alongside AI summary in the detail panel, not below it.
Inline action chips — surface 2–3 AI-suggested next steps as chips in the list cards, not just in the detail panel (PagerDuty pattern).

Questions to Test

Does the MSP operator need a conversational "Ask anything" input in the detail panel, or is the structured recommendation sufficient?
Should the "Patch 8 firewalls" CTA button launch a workflow in NSM, or link to a step-by-step remediation guide?
Is a dependency / attack-path graph (Datadog / Wiz pattern) useful for SAMI Renewals and Policy Gap categories, or primarily for CVE Vulnerabilities?
